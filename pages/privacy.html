Privacy Notice

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly when you browse, shop or create an account with Mawego Bookshop.

This notice explains what information may be collected through our website, why it is used and how you can contact us about your personal information.

Information we collect

When you place an order or use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact telephone number.
  • Your email address, where provided.
  • Delivery address or collection information.
  • Order details, purchased products and transaction references.
  • Account information when you register or sign in.

We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

How we use your information

We use the information collected for legitimate shop operations, including:

  • Processing and managing customer orders.
  • Arranging delivery or shop collection.
  • Sending order confirmations and important updates.
  • Providing customer support and resolving order issues.
  • Managing customer accounts and reward points.
  • Protecting the website against fraud and misuse.
  • Improving our products and shopping experience.

Payments and security

Payments may be processed through supported payment providers, including M-Pesa and card payment services.

Payment providers handle payment information according to their own security and privacy practices. We do not intend to store complete card numbers or card security codes on our website.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to help protect customer information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Orders and customer accounts

We retain relevant order information so that we can process purchases, provide receipts, handle customer enquiries and support order tracking.

If you register an account, information such as your order history and reward points may be associated with that account.

Please keep your account login details secure and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

When information may be shared

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers when necessary to operate the shop.

  • Payment providers to process transactions.
  • Delivery providers to fulfil delivery requests.
  • Email or technology providers to deliver essential services.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Information shared with service providers should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the service being provided.

How long we keep information

We keep personal and order information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil orders, maintain business records, provide support, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or clarification about personal information associated with your account or orders.

You may also ask us to review information that is inaccurate or no longer necessary, subject to applicable record-keeping and legal requirements.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request involving personal information.

Contact Mawego Bookshop

If you have a privacy question, need an order corrected or want to make a request about your personal information, please contact the shop through the available customer support channels.

Need assistance?

Our team can help with questions about your orders, account information and privacy-related concerns.
